## About the Book

*Transformation Menu* is a **cookbook for emotional life and faith**. It is not a success manual. It is a notebook of recipes, a map of flavors, sensations and reflections that turns each day of the week into one dish, with substance and intention.

Each chapter pairs **one dish** with **one life lesson**, **one practical tool** (an agile methodology, framework or technique), **one timeless proverb** and **one Bible verse**. The result is a book that reads like a conversation in a kitchen: warm, funny, sometimes rude, always honest.

**Who it is for:** anyone whose mental kitchen feels messy: pots of worry boiling, spices of anxiety scattered, a smell of "something burning" with no known source. Readers who like practical frameworks, but also want faith, humor and affection.

**What it promises:** to help you *build a purpose with the ingredients you already have*, organize your ideas and act with mastery, without waiting for the perfect pantry.

## The Core Idea

> **Life is a kitchen. You can make something delicious with what you have, as long as you choose well, cook with presence and share the table. And do all of it with faith.**

The book rewrites the obvious, already lived and already known, for the time we are living in. It borrows from three kinds of wisdom:

| Source | What it gives the book |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen and family wisdom** | Everyday sayings, recipes, rituals at the table |
| **Faith** | Scripture, prayer, trust as a "low flame" under everything |
| **Modern methods** | Agile, Scrum, Kanban, Pomodoro, PDCA, OKR, Kaizen, essentialism |

## The Cooking Toolkit: Methodology, Framework, Tool

The book teaches three levels of "utensils", using a kitchen analogy:

| Level | Kitchen analogy | Examples used in the book |
|---|---|---|
| **Methodology** | Your culinary philosophy: *why* you cook a certain way | Agile, the "Culinary of Transformation" |
| **Framework** | A structured recipe: a step-by-step | Scrum, Kanban, Kaizen, and the book's own frameworks |
| **Tool** | A concrete utensil for the daily routine | Pomodoro, PDCA, OKR, the book's checklists and filters |

## The Weekly Cycle

The book is **a cycle, not a straight line**: it starts on Wednesday (the bridge) and ends on Tuesday, which closes the circle and reopens the next Wednesday. Each day has one dish and one verb, and each verb answers the one before.

| # | Day | Dish | Verb | Guiding question | Appendix (tool) | Anchor verse |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Wednesday** | Minas-style *Tutu* (mashed beans) | **Begin** | Can I cook alone? | **App. 1:** The Methodology of Perseverance (Scrum and Kanban) | Romans 12:2 |
| 2 | **Thursday** | Chicken *Bauru* sandwich | **Choose** | Am I suffering for the roast beef I don't have? | **App. 2:** The Divine Focus Framework | Luke 10:41-42 |
| 3 | **Friday** | Mushroom risotto | **Celebrate** | Who am I really cooking for? | **App. 3:** The Purpose Tool | Colossians 3:23 |
| 4 | **Saturday** | *Macarronada da Mama* (family pasta) | **Pacify** | A perfect dish, or a cook at peace? | **App. 4:** The Peaceful Home Tool | Proverbs 17:1 |
| 5 | **Sunday** | Lasagna | **Refine** | Do I do it again, only better? | **App. 5:** The Good Craftsman Methodology | 2 Timothy 2:15 |
| 6 | **Monday** | The lunchbox (*marmita*) | **Organize** | Where do I put things so the week fits? | **App. 6:** The Framework of Order | 1 Corinthians 14:40 |
| 7 | **Tuesday** | Hand-pressed smash burger ("Four Hands") | **Unite** | Who takes me farther? | **App. 7:** The Kitchen Companion Methodology | Ecclesiastes 4:9-10 |

**How the answers chain together:**
*Begin* (courage) → *Choose* (focus) → *Celebrate* (meaning) → *Pacify* (atmosphere) → *Refine* (practice) → *Organize* (system) → *Unite* (community) → back to *Begin*, now with company.

## Anatomy of a Chapter

Every chapter follows the same skeleton, whatever its tone:

1. **Epigraph:** a proverb or quote
2. **Opening** in the tone of the day
3. **The dish** and what it teaches
4. **The Recipe:** ingredients that carry meaning, plus a method with soul
5. **Kitchen Talk:** a dialogue with the people who inspire the author
6. **Pause and Breathing:** a short mindfulness exercise
7. **Challenge of the day**
8. **Activity in the *Be Chef for Do* app**, with a named badge
9. **Provocations:** four to five reflection questions
10. **Appendix:** the day's tool, framework or methodology, with a "Connection to the rest of the menu" block
11. **Bridge** to the next day

## A Different Voice for Each Day

One author, seven moods. The Introduction tells the reader up front that each day has its own voice, so the tone shifts feel like seasoning, not inconsistency.

| Day | Voice | Style marks |
|---|---|---|
| **Wednesday** | Reflective, courageous, down to earth | Bridge and onion metaphors, light humor, quiet faith |
| **Thursday** | **Warm and welcoming**, narrated by the author's mother | Kitchen conversation, short sentences, tender faith, tears and laughter |
| **Friday** | **Irreverent, "bar-table" register** | Swearing, slang, jokes, tables, posh-dish versus street-talk contrast |
| **Saturday** | Big, noisy family table | Many characters, loving chaos |
| **Sunday** | Silent master craftsman | Few words, dry humor, layers |
| **Monday** | Practical coach | Lists, checklists, Scrum and Pomodoro in action |
| **Tuesday** | **Commercial, business-minded** | Executive summary, value proposition, action plan, call to action |

**What never changes:** the chapter skeleton, the kitchen metaphors, faith as a low flame, the invitation to criticism, and the affection for the reader.

## The Ecosystem

The book is the heart of a larger system:

- **Book** (print, e-book, audiobook with a companion PDF of affective pauses and recipes)
- **YouTube channel "Menu da Transformação":** practical demos, deeper discussions of the methodologies, and a lot of humor
- **App "Be Chef for Do":** a daily companion that gamifies the journey with points, levels, "master-chef" badges and rewards. It also introduces basic financial-education concepts hidden in the ingredients. The app was built "with six hands" by the author and two friends.

Example in-app activities already designed: the **Life Onion** visualizer and **Tutu Diary** (Wednesday), the **Essentials Menu** (Thursday), the **Reward Menu** (Friday), the **Brigade Board** (Tuesday).
